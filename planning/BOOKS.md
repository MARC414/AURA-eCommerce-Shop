## Correction to the earlier allow-list

The previous labels "cleared" and "cleared pending a page check" were too strong. An Internet Archive `NOT_IN_COPYRIGHT` metadata field is a useful research lead, not a worldwide rights determination for a particular file. Author dates alone do not settle every edition, translation, illustration, or territory.

## Evidence to record when selecting a book

- Exact title, edition, local path, source URL, author, translator, illustrator/editor where relevant, and publication/contributor dates.
- Rights basis for the relevant territories and the intended activity: full display, download, and/or commercial redistribution.
- Any source licence or attribution conditions and the evidence/date supporting the decision.
- A separate explicit publication status. Do not turn a candidate or filename into an approval.

For Gutenberg material, consult the work's own terms and the [official licence](https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/license.html). Do not apply the earlier blanket instruction to strip branding. Archive download availability is also not permission by itself.
