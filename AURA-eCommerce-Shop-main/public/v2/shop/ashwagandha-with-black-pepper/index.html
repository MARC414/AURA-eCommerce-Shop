Listed as a 60-capsule pack combining Withania somnifera root and Piper nigrum fruit in plant-derived pullulan capsules. A supplied label-artwork PDF states 2 capsules per serving across 30 servings per bottle, alongside 1,300 mg ashwagandha root and 10 mg black pepper fruit per serving.

OPAR highlights one supplier discrepancy openly in the specification table: CJ's automated category field reads 'Powder' while both the narrative listing and supplied label describe 60 capsules, and the stated 113 g (0.25 lb) figure represents gross bottle weight rather than net herbal weight.

Because this is an ingestible botanical supplement, medical benefit claims, stress or cognitive outcomes, and unverified organic certificates are strictly excluded. Sample pricing allows checkout testing only while batch laboratory certificates and destination suitability are checked.

Care & handling

Keep sealed in a cool, dry cabinet out of direct sunlight. Review pack warnings and consult a qualified practitioner if taking medication, pregnant, or breastfeeding.

What OPAR Will Not Claim

The supplied PDF is label artwork. It records ingredient amounts but does not verify the contents of a batch or substantiate certification claims. This 60-capsule listing remains a preview while the evidence is checked.
