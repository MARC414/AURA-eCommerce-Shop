Showpieces · OPAR Construction Sample

The Showpieces room gathers small-batch ornamental objects—turned wooden vessels, natural beeswax candles, and sculptural accents—chosen for tactile restraint rather than seasonal trend cycles.

Handmade vessels carry natural grain variation from ring to ring. When artisan partners are confirmed, each piece page records exact wood species, workshop location, finishing oil, and approximate diameter.

Sample pricing across two vessel diameters gives a realistic mid-ticket anchor to inspect alongside lower-ticket cleansing bars in the shopping bag.

Care & handling

Dust gently with a dry linen cloth. Keep away from radiators and dishwasher moisture; condition lightly with food-safe mineral oil if dry.
