OPAR brand system, v1.0.

Five colours sampled from the approved reference sheet, one recorded exception (antique gold, D-034, dark ground only), documented mixes for everything else, Cormorant Garamond with Manrope, and squared surfaces with pills for actions. This page loads the production token layer, so the values below are the values the site renders. Written guidance lives in docs/BRAND-GUIDELINES.md and docs/PRODUCT-IMAGE-GUIDE.md.

Rule of the sheet. No unreviewed sixth colour, no gradient, no "premium" neutral. If a value is not one of the five below, a stated mix of them, or the recorded gold accent in its four approved places, it does not belong on the site.

The five colours

Sampled at the swatches, not read from their labels — the printed labels on the source sheet all repeat one placeholder value. Contrast is measured, not estimated.

Gold — the secret accent (D-034, 2026-11-11)

Antique gold is a sixth colour with a narrow job. It never fills a surface and never sits on a light ground — it is a reward for interaction, on the dark ground only. Four touches exist in the built site, and no more. Live specimens: select the first line, hover the link, read the hairline, and look at the word.

The colour

Allowed: interaction, micro-detail, display type on ground Never: surface, large fill, text on light, nav, footer, the button

Where it appears — four touches

Touch 4 · A world of conscious living.

Measured 2026-11-11, not assumed. Through the real hero photograph — scrim and brand grade applied, 33,500 samples across the headline band — the gold word holds 6.38:1 median and 4.34:1 at its worst point, on 51–128px display type, so large-text AA holds everywhere. A gold line inside the primary button was rejected: 1.52:1 on the fill, 1.40:1 on the mist hover.

Roles and surfaces

Every surface below is a mix of the ground and Main Text at a stated percentage. Hairline edges are decorative and carry no information on their own.

Ground family

Raised — ground + 7 % text Well — ground − 12 % value (footer) Line — ground + 16 % text

Paper family

Reading surface

Policies, about and help pages swap roles: Main Text becomes the ground and the ground becomes its ink, at 8.59:1 for body copy.

Type

Cormorant Garamond for the voice, Manrope for the interface. The reference did not require a new pairing, and a third family would weaken this one.

Heading · 1.55rem

Body copy sets at 15px on a 1.72 line height and stays inside a 640px measure. Longer lines on a dark ground tire the eye faster than they do on paper.

Actions

The pill is the reference's signature, and the only place a pill appears. One primary action per surface. A partner offer never takes the filled pill, so the visitor can tell a house purchase from an external referral at a glance.

Images: what the layout does

The tile is 4:5, light, and shows the file whole with about 20px of padding. That means the file's own background is what the visitor sees. A light background merges with the tile; a dark or busy one becomes a visible rectangle inside the frame.

Works

  • Portrait or square, 1200–2000px, sRGB, under 250 KB.
  • Plain light background, product filling most of the frame.
  • A printed pack label is part of the product and is allowed.
  • No badges, prices, claims or watermarks added in post.

Does not work

  • Supplier marketing composites: headline type and claim pills baked in.
  • Cut-outs: these files have shadows, props and gradients, so a key leaves a halo.
  • Tinting a product toward the palette — the soap is orange, not green.
  • Inventing a missing view, or an AI image passed off as the product.

Withheld file. The aloe supplier artwork carries an "Anti-Acne" overlay and label line. That is a medicinal claim, so the pixels are not published and the listing shows the placeholder instead. The source URL is kept in data/pilot-products.json as provenance.

Do and don't

Do

  • Use the five colours and the documented mixes.
  • Set text in Main Text or Mist, on the ground or on paper.
  • Reserve the Button colour for the primary action.
  • Label every gap, and write "Price on request" when there is no price.
  • Keep the pill for actions and everything else square.

Don't

  • Set small text in the Accent on the ground (1.97:1).
  • Place the Button fill on paper without its accent edge (2.33:1).
  • Add a sixth colour, a gold, a gradient or a neutral grey.
  • Tint a product photograph to sit inside the palette.
  • Invent prices, ratings, stock, reviews, certifications or health claims.

Measurements quoted here were taken on the rendered pages at 375, 768 and 1440px. Where a rule exists because a pair failed, the failing ratio is shown rather than described.

Button system (T7, settled 2026-11-10)

Four roles, three sizes (default / small), one pill. Measured in the browser against the house spec in docs/BRAND-GUIDELINES.md (§Buttons): the values below are the settled system, not an approximation.

Primary action Secondary Quiet link Small primary Small secondary Inline primary
Compact primary Partner offer → leaves site Quiet text link
Base
Width auto within the container. Full-width in columns because the form column, not the pill, sets the width. One circumference, 999px radius; pill only elsewhere stays square.
Settled values
Height 40px (small) / 56px (default), padding 10px 18px (small) / 17px 22px (default), 11px uppercase Manrope 600, tracking 0.12em, 18px icon. Primary hover lifts to mist (#D0D3C4), never a new hue.
Focus
2px outline, 3px offset, uses the mist ring. Tiles flip the offset inward.

Button text uses 10-11px Manrope uppercase at 600 weight. Hover is restricted to transform and background-color only, and is suppressed under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce via the existing media query.
